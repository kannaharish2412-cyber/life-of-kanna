      Share with your doctor

      See a doctor if

      • Bleeding soaks a pad or tampon every hour for several hours
      • Pain is severe or stops you from daily activity
      • Cycles are shorter than 21 or longer than 35 days, or periods stop for 3+ months
      • Periods last more than 7 days or you feel dizzy or faint

      Cramp relief

      • Child's pose: kneel, sit back on heels, reach forward, breathe slowly for 1 minute.
      • Cat-cow: on hands and knees, arch and round your back with your breath, 10 rounds.
      • Reclining twist: lie on your back, drop both knees to one side, 30 seconds each side.
      • Legs up the wall: lie down with legs raised for 3 to 5 minutes.
      • Heat: a warm pad on the lower belly for 15 to 20 minutes.
